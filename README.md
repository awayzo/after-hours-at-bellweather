# After Hours at Bellweather

**A campus mystery developed by Dr. Malik Stalbert.**

Your CIT 140 field project sounded simple: investigate missing room bookings and recommend a fix. Then a printer issues a reservation in your name for Room 214—a room in a building that closed six years ago. The meeting starts at 2:13 a.m.

Your partner Jules wants to clear a family name. Mina knows which records survived the closure. Theo can tell you what the locked doors actually prove. Malik appears when an investigation goes wrong, and seems to know which question you were about to ask.

Follow the paper trail across Bellweather College, investigate Lantern Woods, and decide whom to trust when the route leads beyond campus. Your character has a reason to recognize places she believes she has never visited.

## Download and play

Download the Windows ZIP from this repository's **Releases** page. Right-click the ZIP and choose **Extract All**. Keep all extracted files together in a writable folder, then launch the included game executable. Do not launch it from inside the ZIP.

This release is a Windows desktop game. SMILE GAME BUILDER is not required for an exported standalone package. A browser, macOS, Chromebook, or phone version is not included.

## Controls

- Arrow keys: move and navigate choices.
- Enter or Z: inspect, speak, advance text, and confirm choices.
- Escape or X: cancel or open the game menu when available.
- Walk onto doorways to travel. You do not need another confirmation press.
- Face a person, shelf, desk, panel, or notice and press Enter to investigate. Clues are placed on physical objects; a floating icon does not identify every clue.
- Use blue-bound casebooks to save, review conversations, inspect your field kit, check points, and read your phone inbox. The HUD also offers clickable shortcuts.

## Food, rest, and your phone

Open **Course score / credit → Find food / charging / inns** for directions at any casebook. Service signs also stand in the Commons and Quad.

From the Commons, take the west campus passage to the Quad. **Lantern House is southwest**. Its desk sells meals, water, and travel supplies. Phone charging is free and takes 45 game minutes. A bed with breakfast and water costs $18 and restores health after eight hours. If you run out of money, ask for the free hardship meal and bunk.

**The Hollow is southeast**. Orin Vale offers an $8 bunk and $4 soup with water. Its free outlet lets you keep your phone while it charges. A cheap bunk restores sleep; it does not provide breakfast or full health recovery. Check your supplies before sleeping. Something about Orin's guest register deserves a second look.

You start with $60, two packed meals, and two waters. Eat about every six game hours, drink every four, and sleep after about sixteen hours awake. Travel and services advance time; reading and dialogue pause it. Prolonged neglect can drain health. An active field placement pays a $45 daily stipend at 8 a.m. Money and course points are separate.

Dangerous detours carry warnings. A failed route can cost time, money, or health. Recovery preserves your evidence and course progress. Save before taking a risk.

## Investigation and up to 50 course points

Reading supplies clues. Reading records and collecting witness dialogue by themselves award no points. You must use evidence in a supported finding and complete applied investigation work.

Chapter One includes an authorized service task: find three components across campus, unlock and assemble an isolated test rack, run a safe diagnostic, and explain the result. Other routes offer equivalent applied work. Optional underground exploration includes a fused circuit, clue-derived terminal code, and a locked archive.

Five categories cap at ten points each. Additional routes and repeat visits cannot raise the overall maximum above 50. Wrong answers can be corrected. The casebook's **Earned points / next steps** page explains what counted and gives leads for unfinished work without revealing puzzle answers.

Point awards display an animated notification. Every increase creates a numbered progress receipt and a BW1 progress code. Review saved award messages through **Phone / inbox**. Local text copies are saved in **Documents / Bellweather Receipts**. Keep the receipt number and code together.

Post discovered items, screenshots, and a brief explanation in **your course Discord**. Include your final points breakdown. Windows + Shift + S captures a screenshot.

The Canvas portal will be developed separately. These receipts record game progress and await verification; they do not post grades automatically. Your instructor will provide submission instructions. Do not submit your Windows save folder or personal information to this repository.

## Save progress and report problems

Save at a casebook before exiting. Keep your game folder when updating until your save has been backed up. For a bug report, include the location, what you were trying to do, the last action, and a screenshot of any error. Avoid posting personal receipt codes publicly.
