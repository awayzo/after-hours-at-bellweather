## The story

Your CIT 140 field project sounded simple: investigate missing room bookings and recommend a fix. Then a printer issues a reservation in your name for Room 214—a room in a building that closed six years ago. The meeting starts at 2:13 a.m.

Your partner Jules wants to clear a family name. Mina knows which records survived the closure. Theo can tell you what the locked doors actually prove. Malik appears when an investigation goes wrong. Your character recognizes places she believes she has never visited.

## The illustrated interface

This version includes the illustrated clock, phone battery/reception, character health, parchment shortcuts, textured reading pages, green terminals, and point-award receipts. Use the casebook or toolbar to review progress and next steps.

## Controls

- Arrow keys / WASD: move. Arrow keys navigate choices.
- Enter / Z: inspect, speak, advance text, and confirm.
- Escape / X: cancel or open the game menu.
- Q / E: rotate the camera. C / V: zoom. K: reset it.
- Walk onto doorways to travel. Face people or objects and press Enter to investigate.

Use blue-bound casebooks to save, review evidence and conversations, read messages, and check course progress. Save before leaving. README.md and START HERE.txt in the game folder include rest, charging, and receipt instructions.

## Course extra credit

Earn up to 50 course points through investigation and reasoning. Open **Casebook → Course score / credit** to review your progress and export a receipt. Receipts are saved in **Documents → Bellweather Receipts**.

Submit the complete receipt and code in your course Canvas extra-credit assignment. Your instructor reviews the work and enters one score manually. The game and comic share one opportunity of up to 50 points. Post discovered items and evidence screenshots in your course Discord; keep personal receipt codes out of public posts.

## Updates and support

Back up your old game folder before updating. The complete EXE stores its files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Bellweather\Windows-v0.3.0. Keep previous game folders and receipts. Start a new case for the updated adventure.

For a bug report, include the game version, Windows version, graphics mode, location, last action, and an error screenshot. Do not post personal saves or full receipt codes publicly.
